Physics Quick Guide

Angle: The launch direction above horizontal. It controls arc shape.

Speed: How fast the projectile starts moving. Higher speed gives longer travel.

Gravity: Downward acceleration from a planet or moon that bends the path.

Range: The total horizontal distance traveled before landing.

Max Height: The highest point reached during flight.

Time of Flight: How long the projectile stays in the air.
